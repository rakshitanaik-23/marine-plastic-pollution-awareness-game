🎮 HOW TO PLAY

⌨️ Controls

  • ⬆️ / W: Swim Up
  • ⬇️ / S: Swim Down
  • ⬅️ / A: Swim Left
  • ➡️ / D: Swim Right
  • ESC / P: Pause Game
  • On-screen D-Pad also available!

🐟 Healthy Food (+10 pts)

  • 🎐 Jellyfish: Sea turtle favorite!
  • 🐟 Coral Fish: Nutritious treat
  • 🌿 Kelp Pod: Pure ocean energy
  • Collect food to reach level threshold early!

🧴 Plastic Hazards (-1 ❤️)

  • Single-use Bottles: Drifting trash
  • Shopping Bags: Choking hazard
  • Snack Wrappers & Cans: Debris
  • 6-Pack Rings: Entanglement
  • Causes screen shake & damage blink.

☣️ Toxic Waste (-2 ❤️❤️)

  • Industrial Barrels: Appear in L4 & L5
  • Glows with radioactive hazard aura
  • Drains 2 full hearts on contact!
  • Dodge with quick directional bursts!

⏱️ Level Progression & Duration

Each level lasts 60 seconds. Advance by either hitting the level score threshold early or surviving all 60 seconds. Accumulate your score across all 5 levels to achieve the title of Ocean Guardian!

🌊 SDG 14: LIFE BELOW WATER

“SDG 14 aims to conserve and sustainably use the oceans, seas and marine resources for sustainable development.”

⚠️ The Problem

Over 14 million tons of plastic enter our oceans annually. Sea turtles frequently mistake plastic bags for jellyfish, causing fatal blockages and toxicity.

💡 The Solution

An interactive educational game demonstrating the visceral effects of plastic pollution, teaching players to recognize hazards and rethink plastic consumption.

🌱 The Impact

Inspires youth and communities toward active conservation: beach cleanups, circular economy practices, single-use plastic bans, and SDG advocacy.

📊 Critical Marine Pollution Facts

  • • Plastics make up 80% of all marine debris found from surface waters to deep-sea sediments.
  • • More than 100,000 marine animals die every year from entanglement and ingestion of ocean plastics.
  • • A single discarded plastic bottle takes upwards of 450 years to break down into microplastics.
